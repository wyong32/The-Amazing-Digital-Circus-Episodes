## About The Amazing Digital Circus

*The Amazing Digital Circus* is a surreal animated web series created by Gooseworx and GLITCH Productions. The story follows humans trapped inside a colorful digital circus run by Caine, an AI ringmaster who turns distress into adventure. Across nine episodes — from the **Pilot** through the finale **Remember** — the show blends comedy, psychological horror, and mystery around themes like abstraction, identity loss, false exits, and survival inside a system that never stops performing.

This site helps viewers watch in order, understand each episode, track character changes, and connect the animation to the popular fan horror visual novel **TADC Mind Games**.

## TADC Mind Games — Quick Guide

**TADC Mind Games** is a fan horror VN, not an official GLITCH product. It borrows the tone of the show — Caine's polished control, Pomni's escape panic, and Kinger's strange clues — and turns them into branching dialogue choices.

**Suggested first four runs**

1. **Blind run** — Learn how the game treats trust, fear, refusal, and delay without a spreadsheet.
2. **Pick a mood** — Replay committed to one angle: trust Caine, defy Caine, help Pomni, or follow Kinger.
3. **Invert one answer** — Change the choice that felt most wrong using opposite emotional logic.
4. **Track the ending pull** — Stay consistent until Caine compliance, defiance, Pomni panic, Kinger clue, or mixed unknown becomes clear.

**Five endings to track first**

| Ending pull | Focus | What to watch for |
|-------------|--------|-------------------|
| Caine Compliance | Trust Caine | Obedience, praise, controlled answers |
| Caine Defiance | Question Caine | Suspicion, refusal, direct challenge |
| Pomni Panic | Help Pomni | Escape pressure, fear, emotional support |
| Kinger Clue | Follow Kinger | Odd hints, slower routes, room details |
| Mixed Unknown | Blend choices | Delay, mixed moods, unclear answers |

For the full route desk, choice signals, and embed, visit [Play Game](https://theamazingdigitalcircus.org/play-game).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this an official GLITCH or Gooseworx website?**  
No. [theamazingdigitalcircus.org](https://theamazingdigitalcircus.org) is an independent fan site. Official animation is credited and embedded from YouTube; editorial analysis and theories are labeled as such.

**Where do I watch the official episodes?**  
Each [Episodes](https://theamazingdigitalcircus.org/episodes) page embeds the official YouTube video and links to Netflix where applicable.

**What is the difference between Episodes and Explained?**  
**Episodes** pages focus on official context — video, release facts, plot summary, and canon notes. **Explained** pages host original commentary videos with deeper scene analysis and labeled speculation.

**Can I play TADC Mind Games on this site?**  
Yes. Go to [Play Game](https://theamazingdigitalcircus.org/play-game) and press **Play Game** to load the browser embed. The iframe does not autoplay until you choose to start.

**How many game endings should I track?**  
Start with five pulls: Caine compliance, Caine defiance, Pomni panic, Kinger clue, and mixed unknown. Details are on the [Play Game](https://theamazingdigitalcircus.org/play-game) route desk.

**Is The Amazing Digital Circus appropriate for children?**  
The show looks colorful but deals with entrapment, panic, abstraction, and body-horror imagery. See the [parents preview](https://theamazingdigitalcircus.org/guides/is-the-amazing-digital-circus-for-kids) for scene-level guidance.

**How are theories handled?**  
Canon is stated as fact. Interpretation is marked as editorial analysis. Unconfirmed ideas are labeled fan theory — especially on Explained pages, character profiles, and the ending guide.
